# Voice

Preserve vocabulary, cadence, formality, technical density, point of view,
humor placement, and structural tendencies from the voice profile
(`writer.voice_get`). Match tendencies, not quirks: one unusual word is
noise, a pattern of concrete verbs is voice.

Never imitate surface tics to signal personality. If the profile is empty,
infer tendencies from approved examples and record them before revising.
Flag conflicts (e.g. audience needs simpler diction than the profile
prefers) instead of silently flattening either side.
